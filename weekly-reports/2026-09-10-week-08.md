## Main learning

- Giraph uses vertices, messages, synchronized supersteps, and voting to halt.
- A job in `ACCEPTED` is waiting for YARN resources; it is not executing the algorithm.
- `Double.MAX_VALUE` represents an unreachable shortest-path value in this example.
- Dependency compatibility can fail at runtime even after compilation succeeds.
