## Important interpretation

The `--max-node-id 100000` option was an ID bound, not a request for exactly
100,000 output vertices. The connected-component filter explains why the
verified result has 9,625 vertices. Six rounds were a first pipeline test,
not the final burning model. With `k` source rounds, the first source has
maximum radius `k-1`, the second `k-2`, and the last source radius `0`.
The six-round test therefore used radii `5,4,3,2,1,0`.
